# UX and design reference

This reference describes the interface evidenced by the current source, not a proposed redesign. Stillpoint is a local-first recovery companion: setup and a recovery date are optional, and the core activities are reflection, writing, meditation, peer-support discovery, and reviewing personal progress. Recovery data is stored in this browser profile on this device; this does not mean it is encrypted. Optional public Community Notes and user-selected external links are exceptions to the otherwise local experience.

## Screens and core journeys

- **First run → Today.** A three-step onboarding offers an introduction and privacy disclosure, optional recovery date/focus/intention, and appearance/accessibility/reminder preferences. The date can be omitted and setup can be skipped. See [Onboarding](../../src/app/Onboarding.tsx) and [routing and app states](../../src/app/App.tsx).
- **Today → a chosen next step.** The dashboard combines an optional recovery timer/date editor, the daily Stillpoint reflection, a Scripture card, NA and Our Daily Bread resource cards, and quick links to writing, Reset, meditation, Garden, and Progress. Reflection actions include marking it, saving it to the journal, bookmarking it, or using device text-to-speech. Community Notes are collapsed until opened; opening loads public hosted notes, and posting requires explicit consent. See [Today](../../src/features/today/TodayPage.tsx), [Community Notes](../../src/features/today/CommunityDiscussion.tsx), and the [reading cards](../../src/features/today/NaDailyReadingCard.tsx), [Our Daily Bread card](../../src/features/today/OdbResourcesCard.tsx).
- **Write and keep personal material.** Journal entries can be created, edited, searched, filtered by type, and exported as JSON. New-entry drafts autosave locally and can be recovered. The Field Guide organizes user-created notes, strategies and difficult moments from journal entries, saved readings, contacts, and resources; discreet display hides sensitive entry details. See [Journal](../../src/features/journal/JournalPage.tsx) and [Field Guide](../../src/features/field-guide/FieldGuidePage.tsx).
- **Pause or practice.** Reset is a short optional 90-second flow: choose a feeling and practice, pause or stop, then optionally record a next step. Meditation offers 2/5/10/20-minute practices, a preview, session pause/resume/restart/end controls, optional voice cues and sound, and local history. Breath holds are off by default. See [Reset](../../src/features/reset/ResetPage.tsx) and [Meditation](../../src/features/meditation/MeditationPage.tsx).
- **Find resources and readings.** Meetings filters bundled local snapshots by fellowship, region, city, day, format, text, and time; official links, temporary share-card selection, optional reminders, and an explicitly loaded map are available where supported. Library tabs cover reflections, Scripture (when shown), principles, meditation, and the Journey Collection. See [Meetings](../../src/features/meetings/MeetingsPage.tsx) and [Library](../../src/features/library/LibraryPage.tsx).
- **Review and personalize progress.** Progress presents recorded activity, milestones, personal goals, a timeline, and charms; the separate token screen renders only the latest reached time milestone, with empty and discreet states. Garden visualizes cumulative chosen activity without a streak penalty; its plants sway gently only while the scene is visible, and the motion stops offscreen, when the document is hidden, or when reduced motion is enabled. Settings groups appearance/accessibility, content, reminders, personal contacts/resources, backup/restore, and permanent deletion. See [Progress](../../src/features/progress/ProgressPage.tsx), [Tokens](../../src/features/progress/CurrentMilestoneTokenPage.tsx), [Garden](../../src/features/garden/GardenPage.tsx), and [Settings](../../src/features/settings/SettingsPage.tsx).

## Visual language and layout

The default palette uses warm ivory and paper surfaces, forest-green text/actions, sage accents, and restrained gold; the logo depicts a seedling above layered hills. Body text uses an Inter/system sans-serif stack, while headings and selected editorial copy use Georgia. Pages share a warm canvas, uppercase tracked eyebrow labels, rounded bordered cards, compact pills, and clear primary/secondary/quiet button hierarchy. Landscape illustrations and calm breathing visuals recur in Today, meditation, and Garden. Five selectable color themes, system/light/dark appearance, and configurable text scale are implemented. Sources: [brand tokens](../../public/assets/branding/brand-tokens.css), [theme presets](../../src/data/themes.ts), [logo](../../public/assets/branding/stillpoint-logo.svg), [shared styles](../../src/styles.css), and the illustrative [screenshots](../media/today.webp), [meditation](../media/meditation.webp), [Library](../media/library.webp), [Progress](../media/progress.webp), and [Settings](../media/settings.webp).

On wide screens, primary navigation is a fixed 248px sidebar and content is capped at 1180px. At 701–900px the sidebar becomes an icon rail. At 700px and below, the sidebar is replaced by a fixed bottom navigation with seven destinations plus Quick Find; labels are hidden by default and can be expanded. Safe-area insets and extra content-bottom space account for the fixed navigation. Multi-column layouts generally collapse at 900px, with additional component-specific narrow-screen rules; the body has a 320px minimum width. Desktop sidebar and mobile label-expansion choices are persisted in local storage. See [Navigation](../../src/app/Navigation.tsx) and breakpoint rules in [shared styles](../../src/styles.css).

## Accessibility affordances

Visible keyboard focus uses a 3px outline; forms generally have associated labels, and icon-only navigation/actions expose accessible names. Active nav links, pressed choices, tab selection, dialogs, result/status updates, timers, and progress indicators use corresponding ARIA attributes or live regions. Dialog focus is moved inside, trapped while open, dismissed with Escape, and restored on close by [useDialogFocus](../../src/app/useDialogFocus.ts). Quick Find supports Ctrl/⌘K, initial search focus, arrow-key result movement, and an announced result count. Settings provide high contrast, reduced motion, larger text, discreet display, and an option to hide meditation visuals; OS reduced-motion preference is also respected. These are implementation affordances, not evidence of a complete WCAG audit. Tokens, motion, route-change focus, tab keyboard support, the opt-in map position and the automated axe and contrast checks are described in [Design tokens, motion and accessibility conventions](tokens-motion-accessibility.md).

## Edge states, limitations, and source-level discrepancies

- Startup has a loading state and a specific local-storage failure state. Unknown routes redirect to Today. Screens include empty states for missing entries, sessions, contacts, resources, or reached tokens; invalid/future recovery dates are rejected. Changing a date preserves saved records, while deleting all local data requires two confirmations. See [App](../../src/app/App.tsx), [Today](../../src/features/today/TodayPage.tsx), and [Settings](../../src/features/settings/SettingsPage.tsx).
- Meeting listings are partial, dated snapshots, not a complete or live directory. Faith-Based demo rows are explicitly unverified and excluded from map pins. Map tiles load only after the user opens the map; pins are approximate, and that choice sends ordinary connection metadata and the viewed map area to OpenStreetMap. Reminder delivery depends on notification permission and browser/device support. See [meeting source notes](../../docs/meeting-sources.md), [Meetings](../../src/features/meetings/MeetingsPage.tsx), and [PWA/offline notes](../../docs/wiki/PWA-and-Offline.md).
- Backups are downloadable, unencrypted JSON; restore previews records before confirmation. Browser storage can be cleared or removed, and the app cannot recover data without a backup. Community Notes are public and hosted online for up to 90 days; the UI discloses what is sent and the limits of moderation. See [Data and Privacy](../../docs/wiki/Data-and-Privacy.md).
- **Faith-neutral preference mismatch:** Settings says this preset hides Scripture on Today, but the current [Today page](../../src/features/today/TodayPage.tsx) renders the Scripture section without checking the preset. The Library hides its Scripture tab and Progress changes its Scripture-related labels/counts; do not assume the Today section is hidden.
- **Quick Find route mismatch:** Quick Find advertises “Explore activity charms” at `/charms`, but [App routes](../../src/app/App.tsx) do not define `/charms`; the catch-all redirects unknown paths to Today. The Journey Collection is available within Progress and Library instead.

This is a source review supplemented by repository screenshots. The screenshots are static illustrations with sample values. Live rendering across browsers/viewports, actual assistive-technology behavior, notification delivery, and offline behavior on installed devices were not independently exercised for this reference.
