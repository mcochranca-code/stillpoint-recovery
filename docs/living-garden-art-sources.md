# Living Garden art: historical inspiration and interpretation notes

The Living Garden starter collection uses three original AI-generated images created for Stillpoint. They are not reproductions, copies, or works by Vincent van Gogh. Each draws on a theme from a Van Gogh painting; the app labels that relationship as inspiration and links to the museum’s collection page. The “From the Eye of the Beholder” panel separates the museum-backed historical context from Stillpoint’s own interpretation of its generated image.

## Sunflowers

The Van Gogh Museum identifies its *Sunflowers* as Vincent van Gogh, Arles, January 1889. The museum says he made sunflower paintings in Arles in 1888 and 1889 and wrote that they communicated “gratitude.” That is historical context for the source painting, not a quotation about Stillpoint’s generated image.

Source: [Van Gogh Museum collection: Sunflowers](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0031v1962).

## Almond Blossom

The Van Gogh Museum identifies *Almond Blossom* as Vincent van Gogh, Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, February 1890. The museum describes the early spring bloom as a symbol of new life and records that Van Gogh gave the painting to Theo and Jo after their son Vincent Willem was born.

Source: [Van Gogh Museum collection: Almond Blossom](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0176v1962).

## The Bedroom

The Van Gogh Museum identifies *The Bedroom* as Vincent van Gogh, Arles, October 1888. The museum says the bright colors were meant to express repose or sleep and that Van Gogh deliberately flattened the room’s perspective to make it resemble a Japanese print.

Source: [Van Gogh Museum collection: The Bedroom](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0047v1962).

## Attribution language

Use wording such as “Original AI-generated artwork for Stillpoint, inspired by Vincent van Gogh’s *Sunflowers* (1888–1889).” Never imply that Van Gogh created, approved, or endorsed the image, or that an AI-generated work was contributed by an AA/NA member. Member-submitted art and stories are not currently collected or hosted in the app.
