# Meeting-directory sources and coverage

The app bundles public schedule facts locally; no meeting feed is requested at runtime. The original Stanislaus County AA/NA snapshot was checked on **October 23, 2026**. The 41 regional example records below were checked against the official sources on **October 26, 2026**; two duplicate schedules are shown once where the same row is also in the workbook snapshot. The workbooks were user-validated and checked **October 26, 2026**. The county meeting workbook preserves its source title/URL, modality, access/format, timezone and verification notes. The separate online/Zoom workbook preserves the listed schedule, source, scope, timezone and verification note but intentionally omits direct meeting invitations, IDs, passcodes and join instructions from the public build. All bundled schedules are partial snapshots, not exhaustive county inventories or live schedules. Confirm time, venue, access and current status with the source before attending.

## October 26, 2026 workbook snapshot

The app imports all **233 rows** from the workbook’s `All Meetings` sheet: Alameda County (50), Fresno County (18), Los Angeles County (50), Mariposa County (24), Merced County (78), Stanislaus County (3), and Tulare County (10). The workbook labels its extract substantial and verified, but explicitly says it is **not exhaustive**. These row counts describe only the workbook rows, not official county totals. SMART Recovery appears alongside AA, NA and FA. Online-only rows are kept for offline browsing, but do not receive map pins. Credential details remain on official source pages and are not copied into the app.

The workbook’s own coverage notes are material to interpreting absence: Fresno’s quarterly NA graphic did not reliably associate some rows with weekdays, so those ambiguous meetings were omitted; Mariposa’s NA schedule had a similar weekday ambiguity. Merced had no verified in-county, in-person FA listing in the research snapshot; any national/online option must not be mistaken for a Merced-hosted meeting. The workbook says its Stanislaus rows are selected examples, not the full AA or NA directory. Tulare’s SMART results are online national options rather than evidence of a local venue, and no local FA row was established. Alameda’s extract is only a subset of a much larger AA directory; Los Angeles is also a partial extract of very large AA and NA directories. The app preserves per-county limitation notes from the workbook and does not treat an empty fellowship filter as proof that no meeting exists.

All workbook rows are bundled for offline filtering by fellowship, county, weekday, city, modality, text and listed start time. The original time and timezone qualification are preserved; the app does not convert schedule times. A source record’s presence does not prove continued operation. Open its cited organizer listing to confirm current details immediately before attending.

### Workbook address map

The 166 workbook rows marked in-person or hybrid yielded **85 unique physical-address queries** to the U.S. Census Geocoder `Public_AR_Current` batch benchmark on October 26, 2026. Fifty-five returned `Match` + `Exact`, representing 52 distinct map points; 27 returned `Match` + `Non_Exact`, and 3 returned no match. Only the 55 exact address results are in the bundled coordinate manifest. Hybrid listings with an exact physical address can be pinned; online-only entries and non-exact or unmatched addresses remain in the offline directory without a map pin. The Census API documents that returned latitude/longitude is calculated along an address range, so a marker is approximate and not a surveyed venue entrance. [Census Geocoding Services API](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html)

These coordinates were prepared once and bundled; no address or meeting data is sent to Census at runtime. Stillpoint does not request device location or transmit meeting-search text or filters. Leaflet map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap only after the user chooses “Load interactive map,” which reveals the viewed map area and ordinary connection metadata to the tile service. Meeting markers link to each official schedule. Shared coordinates are grouped.

## October 26, 2026 partial online / hybrid snapshot

The user-provided `Online_Zoom_Recovery_Directory_Partial_2026-10-26.xlsx` contributes all **157 source rows** from its `Online & Hybrid` sheet: 119 online and 38 hybrid records across Fresno (41), Merced (32), Stanislaus (30), Alameda (17), Los Angeles (16), Mariposa (11), and Tulare (10). The rows include AA (109), NA (30), SMART Recovery (13), and FA (5). The app deduplicates a schedule already represented in an earlier local snapshot when its weekday/time, fellowship, mode and source or venue match; each source row remains represented in the static module, while duplicate schedules are shown only once.

The workbook is explicitly **partial**. Fresno, Merced and Stanislaus have a focused online/Zoom pass dated October 26; the workbook says the other four counties retain earlier online/hybrid rows and were not covered by the focused pass. Its seven county-status notes appear in the app. Some listings are broad/national online options shown under a county search context, not groups hosted there; the app preserves and displays the workbook’s “Host city / scope” separately. Listed times and timezone qualifications remain unconverted. Direct Zoom invite URLs, meeting IDs, passcodes and join instructions are not bundled in the public app; each card links to its cited official detail page for current access information. Online-only records never receive map pins. Hybrid venues can appear only if a previously bundled physical address has a Census `Match` + `Exact` coordinate; the app does not geocode these rows at runtime.

## Included offline samples

**Fresno County — Alcoholics Anonymous (AA).** Eight example listings are indexed from the Greater Fresno Area Intergroup Association (GFAIA): [official meeting finder](https://www.fresnoaa.org/meetings/) and [meeting index](https://www.fresnoaa.org/locations/). The public index is organized by region/city rather than county, so it does not supply a reliable county total or prove that every county listing is included. Its reviewed pages did not show a bulk export or explicit data-reuse licence.

**Fresno County — Narcotics Anonymous (NA).** Eight example listings are indexed from the [Central California Narcotics Anonymous meeting schedule](http://www.centralcalna.org/na-meetings.html). The local site links a printable schedule for October–December 2026; it is not a structured export and covers a broader region without a county filter. NA World Services says it does not maintain a central in-person meeting database and directs members to local NA communities. Confirm schedule changes with the local source.

**Mariposa County — AA.** Eight example listings are indexed from the [Mariposa AA Fellowship schedule](https://www.aamariposafellowship.org/page16.html). The local site includes Bootjack, in-town and Yosemite Valley schedules, but its public pages overlap and are not county-filtered. The Bootjack/local schedule was marked updated July 10, 2026; a separate in-town schedule reviewed was last updated in 2016, and Yosemite listings may overlap. No unique county total can be reliably inferred. No Mariposa County NA listing was verified in the official local/regional sources reviewed; this is not evidence that the county has no NA meetings.

**Merced County — AA.** Eight examples are indexed from the [Central Valley Intergroup (CVIAA) directory](https://cviaa.org/aa/meetings/?region=merced). CVIAA exposes a current directory feed tagged to a “Merced” region, but that region includes places outside Merced County; its 33 region-tagged records must not be read as a county count. The directory and printable listing disagree on the Beachwood venue address; this sample uses the address currently shown in the live meeting record and flags the conflict on the card. Verify before traveling.

**Merced County — NA.** Eight examples are indexed from the [SVGNA local-area schedule](https://svgna.org/svgna-meeting-schedule), whose printable schedule was dated October 25, 2026. The source includes multiple communities in Merced County but is an area schedule, not a county-complete directory. Its city-matched row count is not a claim of full county coverage.

**Food Addicts in Recovery Anonymous (FA).** One Fresno listing—Central Valley Church of Christ, Room 6, Tuesday at 6:30 PM—is indexed from its [official meeting page](https://www.foodaddicts.org/meeting/1250), checked October 26, 2026. The official [FA meeting finder](https://www.foodaddicts.org/find-a-meeting) does not expose a county selector or advertised stable bulk export. No Mariposa or Merced County FA listing was verified in the checked sources. These gaps do not imply no meeting exists.

The county/fellowship counts above describe only the examples bundled here; they are not official totals. The local directories reviewed did not publish clear open-data licences or explicit permission for offline app republication. The app includes only the minimum factual fields needed to find the sample meetings, avoids directory prose and logos, attributes and links each record, and does not imply affiliation. No legal conclusion or blanket reuse permission is asserted. If an organization requests a correction or removal, the snapshot should be updated.

## Privacy, maps and source links

The directory and filters work offline. Stillpoint does not request location, record attendance, or send recovery data, search text or filter values to meeting organizations. Only an explicit tap on a source link opens an external site, which may receive ordinary connection metadata. The optional map includes the exact Census address-range matches from both the older Stanislaus snapshot and the October 26, 2026 regional snapshot: 23 distinct regional pins represent 28 example listings across Fresno, Mariposa, and Merced counties. Seven regional non-exact matches and one unmatched address remain unpinned. The Census coordinates are not surveyed venue entrances. Map tiles are loaded on demand from OpenStreetMap and reveal the viewed area to that service; see its [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and [attribution guidance](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The Stanislaus County AA sample comes from [Central Valley Intergroup](https://cviaa.org/aa/meetings/); the local [Delta Intergroup schedule](https://www.aadelta.org/groupschedules?zone=12) directs people to CVIAA for Stanislaus schedules. The Stanislaus NA snapshot comes from [SVGNA](https://svgna.org/svgna-meeting-schedule). Listings the source explicitly marks temporarily closed are omitted. NA format abbreviations follow the source key.

These are independent peer-support directories, not endorsements. Stillpoint does not use AA, NA or FA logos or claim affiliation. Meeting details and accessibility can change. Check with the source before relying on any listing.


## Regional map coordinates — checked October 26, 2026

The regional map coordinate snapshot was built from 35 Census Geocoder batch input rows representing the 40 in-person Fresno, Mariposa, and Merced regional meeting records. The official `Public_AR_Current` address-range benchmark (benchmark ID 4) returned 27 `Match, Exact`, 7 `Match, Non_Exact`, and 1 `No_Match` result. Only `Match` plus `Exact` results are eligible for the bundled coordinate manifest; non-exact and unmatched addresses remain available in the local directory but have no map pin. A geocoder input row is not a meeting count: repeated listings at a shared venue may share one map point.

The U.S. Census Bureau explains that its coordinates are interpolated along potential TIGER/MAF address ranges. They are approximate street-range positions and are not surveyed building entrances. The Census Geocoder was used only to prepare this public venue snapshot; the app does not geocode at runtime and never submits user-entered searches or device location. Confirm current meeting access and exact entrances with each official source before travel. [Census Geocoding Services API](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html) [Census Geocoder FAQ](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/FAQ_for_Census_Bureau_Public_Geocoder.pdf)
