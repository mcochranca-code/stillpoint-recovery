# Stanislaus County meeting-list sources

The in-app AA/NA directory is a static snapshot checked on **October 23, 2026**. Meeting facts are bundled with the application so the list can work offline; the app does not request device location, contact either fellowship, or fetch schedule changes in the background. A source link opens only after the user chooses it. The destination may receive ordinary connection metadata; Stillpoint does not attach a recovery profile, sobriety date, journal text, search history, or attendance record.

The Alcoholics Anonymous listings come from [Central Valley Intergroup’s meeting directory](https://cviaa.org/aa/meetings/). The [Delta Intergroup schedule](https://www.aadelta.org/groupschedules?zone=12) explicitly directs people to Central Valley Intergroup for Stanislaus County schedules. The dataset includes records from the Stanislaus communities named by the local listing and omits entries the source explicitly labels as temporarily closed.

The Narcotics Anonymous listings come from the [SVGNA local-area schedule](https://svgna.org/svgna-meeting-schedule), which links to a printable schedule in its October 2026 uploads. NA World Services says it does not maintain a database of in-person NA meetings and directs visitors to their local NA community for current information; see the [NAWS meeting finder](https://na.org/meetingsearch/find-na/).

Food Addicts in Recovery Anonymous (FA) has an official [live meeting finder](https://www.foodaddicts.org/find-a-meeting), checked on **October 24, 2026**. Its finder supports location, meeting type, language, weekday and time-of-day criteria. The app’s FA filter links to that source but does not copy or cache FA listings in its Stanislaus snapshot. Therefore, an empty local FA filter means only that this app has no bundled FA records; it is not evidence that no FA meetings are available nearby. Search criteria are entered on the official site only after the user opens the external link.

These links are convenience references, not live feeds, endorsements, or guarantees that a meeting still takes place at the listed time or location. Confirm with the official directory before traveling. The app does not use AA, NA, or FA logos and does not claim affiliation. Only basic public meeting details are shown; format abbreviations are those used by the source and may require consulting its key.
