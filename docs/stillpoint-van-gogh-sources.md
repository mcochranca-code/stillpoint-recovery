# Van Gogh museum source notes for Living Garden

The app will host original, AI-generated art for Stillpoint, not reproductions of these historical paintings. Describe the images as inspired by themes and brushwork, and never attribute the generated files to Vincent van Gogh. Present the museum facts below as historical inspiration in a clearly separate “From the eye of the beholder” panel.

- Sunflowers: Vincent van Gogh, Arles, January 1889. The Van Gogh Museum says the sunflower paintings were made in Arles in 1888 and 1889, and Van Gogh wrote they communicated “gratitude.” Source: https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0031v1962
- Almond Blossom: Vincent van Gogh, Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, February 1890. The museum says almond blossoms were a favorite subject and an early-spring symbol of new life; the painting was a gift to his brother Theo and sister-in-law Jo after the birth of their son. Source: https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0176v1962
- The Bedroom: Vincent van Gogh, Arles, October 1888. The museum says the bright colors were meant to express repose or sleep, and the perspective was deliberately flattened to resemble a Japanese print. Source: https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/collection/s0047v1962
